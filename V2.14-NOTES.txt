Aura Digital Intelligence v2.14 — Stability, Social Preview, SEO & Publisher Refresh

BACKEND STABILITY
- Keeps the locked verification requirements: minimum 2 independent sources, confidence 75+, no unverified/conflicted claims.
- Keeps writer threshold 85, originality guard, final quality minimum 90, and daily target 2.
- Adds one bounded article quality-polish pass for verified stories whose first draft misses structural writing targets.
- Requeues approved stories that have no article after a transient interruption.
- Writer queue no longer treats an old completed job as a permanent blocker when the article is missing.
- Final quality gate rechecks strong legacy REVIEW articles after a cooldown and always evaluates the newest content.
- Repairs missing article_sources rows from the approved fact-check evidence before final quality evaluation.
- Keeps embedded Sources links as an additional source-count fallback.
- Removes the Pexels-only restriction from image backfill so Openverse can backfill missing article images.
- Adds a bounded recovery mode after 10:00 Fiji time when the 2/day publication target is still unmet: wider candidate pool, small priority fallback, and capped extra verification budget. Verification thresholds are NOT lowered.
- Adds GET /diagnostics for a simple aggregate pipeline status without exposing secrets or article text.

PUBLIC SITE
- Light theme is now the default. Reader choice is still remembered in local storage.
- Facebook/WhatsApp/Twitter article metadata now puts the real featured article image first, with the branded OG card as fallback.
- Dynamic article OG fallback now includes the featured image when available.
- Article NewsArticle structured data uses the real article image and clearer publisher/isPartOf markup.
- Home page adds WebSite and Organization structured data.
- RSS includes article images through Media RSS.
- Public copy no longer promotes the newsroom as autonomous/automated or talks about AI providers/processes.
- Aura Digital Fiji placement is redesigned into a clearer publisher-studio module with direct service routes, while duplicate article-side promotion was removed.

3. No new Supabase migration is required for v2.14.

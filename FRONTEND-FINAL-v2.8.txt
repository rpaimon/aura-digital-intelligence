MAIN FIXES
- Restored wide desktop canvas for homepage, news archive, topic pages and article pages.
- Long headlines now use length-aware responsive sizing on mobile and desktop.
- Homepage lead uses screen width more efficiently and surfaces additional important stories above the fold.
- Top stories are compact horizontal/editorial headline blocks.
- Article pages use the desktop right column for Aura Digital Fiji, related stories, latest intelligence and publication trust information.
- Mobile remains full width and compact.
- Dark is the default theme; Light is optional and remembered after a visitor chooses it.
- Social previews now use the standard article /opengraph-image route with 1200x630 branded cards.
- Removed the custom /social-card dependency.
- Facebook/WhatsApp/social crawlers remain explicitly allowed in robots rules.
- Public indexing now defaults ON unless NEXT_PUBLIC_PUBLIC_INDEXING_ENABLED is explicitly set to false.
- Expanded sitemap coverage and kept Google News sitemap generation.
- Added article author/publisher metadata, BreadcrumbList JSON-LD and stronger NewsArticle structured data.
- Admin pages explicitly noindex.

SOCIAL PREVIEW NOTE
Facebook and WhatsApp can cache an older failed preview. After Vercel deploy:
1. Test a fresh/new article URL first.
2. For an already-shared Facebook URL, use Facebook Sharing Debugger and request a new scrape.
3. WhatsApp may take time to refresh a previously cached URL; a newly published URL is the best test.
